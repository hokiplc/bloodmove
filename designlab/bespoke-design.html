Our Approach

Engineered for Your Exact Requirements

Off-the-shelf thermal packaging rarely delivers optimal performance. Polar DesignLab designs around your specific payload — its geometry, thermal mass, and criticality — not around a standard box size.

  • Payload characterisation and thermal mass calculation
  • Temperature range definition (ambient, +2–8°C, -20°C, -80°C)
  • Transit duration and worst-case scenario modelling
  • Material selection: VIP, PU foam, PCM, gel packs
  • Geometric optimisation for payload fit and void minimisation
  • Prototype build and iterative refinement
