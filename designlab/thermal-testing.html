Testing Capability

Validate Before You Ship

Polar DesignLab operates temperature-controlled test chambers capable of simulating seasonal ambient profiles, extreme scenarios, and custom duration profiles matched to your real-world transit conditions.

  • Summer profile: +35°C ambient simulation
  • Winter profile: -20°C ambient simulation
  • Extended duration tests: up to 120 hours and beyond
  • Data-logged internal payload temperature throughout
  • ISTA 7D protocol support
  • Bespoke protocol design for unusual routes or payloads
